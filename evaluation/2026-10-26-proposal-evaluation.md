# Saint Ann Parish Facilities Proposal Evaluation
**Date:** October 26, 2026  
**Repository:** Jeffrey-Fisher-GitHub/SAP-Facilities  
**Status:** Provisional evaluation for clarification / Best and Final Offer (BAFO). Not an award recommendation until mandatory documentation and scope clarifications are received.

## Executive recommendation

Saint Ann should **not award either lawn or snow work from the proposals exactly as submitted**. Both firms submitted useful pricing and operating detail, but neither proposal package fully satisfies the Parish RFP response requirements for references, dedicated staffing/equipment deployment, current insurance/licensing evidence, exceptions/subcontractors, and sample service/QC reporting.

### Provisional ranking
| Service | Rank | Firm | Provisional score | Recommendation |
|---|---:|---|---:|---|
| Lawn / grounds | 1 | Jastrem Premium Landscapes | **64/100** | Preferred finalist **only after revised scope and price** |

| Snow / ice | 1 | Jastrem Premium Landscapes | **72/100** | Preferred finalist subject to SLA, deicing, and pricing clarifications |
| Snow / ice | 2 | Bates Landscaping | **62/100** | Viable alternative if event pricing proves cheaper under common scenarios and contract terms are revised |

Scores are based only on evidence contained in the submitted proposal documents and are intentionally conservative where required RFP evidence is absent.

# 2. Evaluation method

The Parish RFP weights were applied:

| Evaluation factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Relevant cemetery / institutional experience | 20% |
| Staffing, equipment and service capacity | 20% |
| Site-specific operating plan and quality controls | 20% |
| Pricing and overall value | 25% |
| References, insurance, licensing and safety program | 10% |
| Reporting, communication and environmental practices | 5% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

A missing required item is treated as **unsubstantiated**, not assumed to exist.

### Key pricing

Quoted extras include weed control ($606.24/year), fall/winter pruning ($1,010.40), and four fertilization applications ($924.76).

### Merits
- Clear line-item pricing and number of mowing visits.
- 26 campus mowing cycles is a reasonable starting frequency for a high-visibility institutional property.
- Proposal recognizes school/playground, river-stone areas, church volunteer plantings, and holiday appearance priorities.
- Four account-manager inspections at no separate charge is a useful QC feature.

### Shortfalls

- No current COI, pesticide credentials, references, or sample service/QC report are incorporated into the proposal.
- No Holy Family School arrival/dismissal safety window is incorporated.
- Vendor terms say Bates' agreement controls in a conflict. Saint Ann's RFP/final addendum should control instead.
- Contract boilerplate includes 2.5% monthly late charges (30% annualized), a 25% minimum collection charge, broad extra-work triggers, vendor-controlled material substitutions, and evergreen renewal language.

### Campus assessment
**66/100 as a stand-alone campus proposal**, subject to the contract-term corrections above.

## 3.2 Bates — Cemeteries (`bates 01`.pdf`)

### Key pricing
| Service | Visits | Seasonal price |
|---|---:|---:|
| Spring cleanup | 1 | $606.24 |
| Regular turf mowing | 20 | $24,249.60 |
| Field/meadow mowing | 2 | $404.16 |
| Fall cleanup 1 | 1 | $2,424.96 |
| **Base total** | | **$27,684.96** |

Quoted extras include four fertilization treatments ($6,154.24) and a second fall cleanup ($2,424.96).

### Merits
- Significant cost savings versus Jastrem's current full-cemetery mowing model.
- Explicit biweekly cemetery mowing concept.
- Recognizes cemetery holiday visitation as high priority.
- Provides a low-cost meadow/field mowing alternative.
- Detailed general descriptions for turf, cleanup, weed control and fertilization.

### Critical shortfall — natural/meadow area is too large
Bates expressly recommends keeping the front entrance and roadside areas neat while moving **upper fields** toward meadow/field mowing only twice annually. It also states the goal is to reduce regular mowing and switch upper fields to meadow mowing.

That is a **scope change**, not merely a cheaper method of performing the Parish RFP. Saint Ann should not accept a contractor-selected conversion of substantial cemetery turf to natural grass.

### Required correction
- Parish must define the natural-grass areas on an attached map.
- All areas not specifically colored/identified as a natural zone remain regular-maintenance turf.
- Bates may not expand a natural zone without a written change order signed by the Parish.
- Entrance areas, roadway edges, sight triangles, active burial areas, pedestrian/visitor corridors and designated monument areas remain maintained turf even when adjoining land is naturalized.
- Natural areas must still receive litter/debris pickup, invasive/noxious weed control, boundary trimming and enough seasonal mowing to remain safe, intentional and compliant with municipal requirements.

### Cemetery assessment
**56/100 as submitted**, primarily because the proposed meadow conversion materially changes the Parish's intended standard.

### Merits
- One integrated proposal covers all Parish properties.
- Separates mowing price for each location, which supports later scope adjustments.
- Mowing method uses the agronomically stronger **one-third blade rule** rather than a simple fixed interval.
- Includes an extensive four-round fertilizer program.
- Cleanup and pruning are integrated into the package.
- Current scope appears to maintain the cemetery more intensively than Bates, so the price is more directly associated with a higher maintenance standard.

### Shortfalls
- Current **$76,581.50** service subtotal is about **$18,700.84 (32.3%) above** the roughly expanded Bates comparison, before exact scope normalization.
- Current proposal maintains too much of Saint Ann Cemetery at regular-mowing intensity for the Parish's desired revised plan.
- No mapped acreage/zone schedule accompanies the price.
- No dedicated staffing and equipment assignment by property.
- Required RFP references, sample QC report, current COI and pesticide credentials are not supplied in an award-ready package.
- Billing is in advance and requires a valid credit card to remain on file.
- Fuel surcharge language activates at gasoline price thresholds and should be replaced with an objective, capped, documented adjustment mechanism or removed.
- 1.5% monthly late charge (18% annualized) and other vendor terms should not override Parish terms.

### Mandatory revised cemetery scope
The revised Jastrem proposal should make the **Parish-designated first half of Saint Ann Cemetery a natural-grass zone**, with the precise boundary shown on a signed site map.

The phrase "first half" must not remain verbal or ambiguous. The final contract map should establish:
1. the exact natural-zone boundary;
2. maintained-mowing buffers along roadways, entrances and visitor routes;
3. active burial/ceremonial areas that remain regularly maintained;
4. sightline/fire/access clearance areas;
5. natural-zone seasonal mowing frequency and invasive-weed obligations.

Jastrem should provide a **specific dollar credit/reduction** from the current $39,866.40 Saint Ann Cemetery mowing line. The Parish should require both:
- regular-turf unit rate ($/acre or $/mapped zone/visit); and
- natural/meadow unit rate ($/acre or $/mapped zone/cycle).

### Jastrem lawn provisional score: **64/100**

### Lawn ranking
**1. Jastrem — 64/100, conditional.** Better overall scope alignment and integrated technical program, but price must materially decline after the Saint Ann Cemetery natural-area redesign.

**2. Bates — 61/100.** Better price/value on paper, but the current cemetery proposal naturalizes too much area. Bates remains a serious finalist if it reprices the exact same Parish-approved zone map used for Jastrem.

# 5. Mandatory RFP compliance gaps for BOTH firms

Before award, require a single response package containing:

- completed Parish Contractor Questionnaire and Bid Form;
- ownership, years in business and primary/24x7 operational contacts;
- cemetery and institutional experience narrative;
- exact staffing and equipment committed to each Parish location;
- service calendar and weather-delay/recovery plan;
- three comparable references, preferably including cemetery and school/church/institutional clients;
- current Certificates of Insurance;
- applicable Pennsylvania pesticide credentials for lawn chemical applications;
- complete pricing schedule with all locations and optional services;
- complete exceptions/exclusions/assumptions list;
- all proposed subcontractors and their roles;
- redacted sample service report / QC checklist.

A proposal missing a mandatory safety, insurance, licensing or scope-map item should be considered **non-responsive until cured**.

# 6. Improvements to the Parish evaluation process

## 6.1 Add pass/fail gates before weighted scoring
Require these before price scoring:
1. signed acceptance of final scope maps;
2. COI meeting Parish/insurer requirements;
3. applicable pesticide credentials;
4. three references;
5. staffing/equipment plan;
6. signed list of exceptions;
7. snow cemetery 36-hour SLA;
8. school safety/arrival/dismissal acceptance.

## 6.2 Normalize evaluated price
Do not score quoted totals directly.

For lawn, evaluate a common basket:
- exact mapped acres/zones;
- fixed number/range of regular mowing cycles;
- natural-zone cycles;
- spring cleanup;
- two fall cleanups if desired;
- pruning;
- identical fertilizer/weed-control option;
- identical bed/mulch scope.

For snow, create three modeled winters:
- light;
- typical;
- heavy.

Apply each bidder's event/seasonal rates, ordinary deicing assumptions and stated surcharges to the same scenarios.

Recommended price score:
**Lowest evaluated common-scope cost / Bidder evaluated common-scope cost × 25 points.**

## 6.3 Use scoring anchors
For every non-price category:
- 0 = absent / unacceptable
- 1 = materially deficient
- 2 = partially responsive
- 3 = meets requirement
- 4 = exceeds requirement with evidence
- 5 = exceptional, measurable and contractually committed

This prevents subjective score inflation.

# 7. Negotiation position

The Parish should conduct one clarification meeting with each finalist, then issue the **same written BAFO/addendum to both**.

The objective is not to make Bates look like Jastrem or Jastrem look like Bates. It is to make both price the **same Parish-defined outcome**.

### Lawn target
- Jastrem: reduce Saint Ann Cemetery mowing scope by converting the Parish-defined first half to natural grass and provide a transparent credit.
- Bates: reduce the size of Bates' proposed natural/meadow area so it matches exactly the same Parish-approved map.
- Both: price the same natural zone, same regular turf, same cleanup, same chemical option and same service standards.

### Snow target
- Jastrem: preserve predictable seasonal pricing, but make deicing inclusions, SLAs and extraordinary/T&M triggers explicit.
- Bates: offer a parish-wide seasonal cap or not-to-exceed alternative in addition to event rates; accept the same service SLAs.

# 8. Award recommendation framework

## Lawn
Do not award until BAFO. If Jastrem provides a meaningful natural-zone credit and moves materially closer to Bates on normalized price while retaining the stronger full-service scope, Jastrem should remain the preferred finalist.

If Bates agrees to the Parish-defined limited natural zone, prices the remaining cemetery turf at regular service, and accepts the Parish contract terms while retaining a material price advantage, Bates can overtake Jastrem.

## Snow
Jastrem is the stronger current proposal because its seasonal accumulation model is easier to budget and its campus service concept is closer to the Parish's operational need. Award should still depend on confirming deicing inclusions, the cemetery 36-hour requirement, school opening SLA, equipment commitment and no-double-billing rules.

Bates remains a viable negotiating alternative, especially if it provides a parish-wide seasonal cap and removes high-volatility extras for ordinary winter conditions.
