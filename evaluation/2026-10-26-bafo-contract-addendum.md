# Saint Ann Parish — BAFO and Contract Addendum Requirements
**Purpose:** Issue the same requirements to Bates and Jastrem before final award.

## 1. Order of precedence
In any conflict, the documents control in this order:
1. executed Saint Ann Parish final contract/addendum;
2. Saint Ann Parish RFP and issued addenda/site maps;
3. vendor BAFO;
4. vendor proposal;
5. vendor standard terms.

No vendor term may state that its standard agreement supersedes the Parish RFP/addendum.

## 2. Lawn site maps and natural-grass zones
Each property must have a signed map identifying:
- **Zone A — manicured/high-visibility turf**
- **Zone B — standard/reduced-frequency maintained turf**
- **Zone C — natural/meadow turf**

No zone may be changed without written Parish approval.

### Saint Ann Cemetery
The Parish intends for the **Parish-designated first half of Saint Ann Cemetery to be Zone C natural grass under the revised Jastrem scope**. Bates must use the **same final map** in its BAFO; Bates' current proposal naturalizes too much area.

The signed map must preserve regular maintenance for:
- entrances and public-facing frontage;
- roadway shoulders/buffers;
- sight triangles and access routes;
- active burial/funeral areas;
- designated visitor/monument corridors;
- fire/emergency access.

Natural areas remain subject to:
- litter/debris pickup during normal visits;
- invasive/noxious weed control;
- boundary trimming;
- seasonal field mowing sufficient to maintain intentional appearance and municipal compliance;
- emergency cutting when needed for access, funerals or safety.

Require separate unit prices for:
- regular turf $/acre/visit;
- reduced-frequency turf $/acre/visit;
- natural/meadow turf $/acre/cycle.

## 3. Lawn service standards
- Campus: approximately 26 seasonal mowing cycles, adjusted by growth/weather only with Parish approval when a material interval is missed.
- No more than 14 calendar days between needed cuts without written explanation/approval.
- Target manicured turf height: 3.0–3.5 inches unless a mapped turf type requires otherwise.
- Follow the one-third blade rule.
- String trim/edge required on each completed mowing visit in public-facing and pedestrian areas.
- Blow clippings from walks, curbs, entrances, monuments, markers and paved areas before leaving.
- No visible clumps left after service.
- No mowing on saturated turf where rutting or damage is likely; delayed service is rescheduled promptly.
- Cemetery crews must use equipment and trimming methods that prevent damage to markers/monuments.

## 4. Holy Family School safety
No mowing, trimming, blowing or routine chemical application in immediate school arrival/dismissal areas during:
- **7:40–8:15 AM**
- **2:40–3:15 PM**
on school days.

Chemical applications must comply with label requirements and applicable Pennsylvania school/playground notice, licensing and restricted-entry requirements.

## 5. Cemetery funeral protocol
Vendor must:
- coordinate with the Parish for scheduled funerals/burials;
- suspend disruptive work in the affected area before and during services;
- restore clean access before visitors arrive;
- provide an emergency grounds-response procedure and unit rates.

## 6. Damage responsibility
Vendor must document pre-existing damage at mobilization. New damage caused by vendor operations to:
- monuments/markers;
- curbs/pavement;
- fencing;
- buildings;
- landscaping;
- utilities/irrigation known or reasonably visible
must be reported promptly and repaired/restored at vendor expense, subject to normal verification.

## 7. Lawn reporting / QC
After each visit provide an electronic record showing:
- date/time;
- property;
- crew;
- services/zones completed;
- chemical/material applications;
- skipped areas and reason;
- damage/safety observations;
- photographs for exceptions.

Require account-manager/QC walkthrough at least monthly during peak season and correction of material service defects within 24–48 hours.

## 8. Snow service levels

### Main campus
Critical areas include parking/drive lanes, ADA routes, building entrances, emergency/fire access and primary sidewalks.

On Holy Family School days:
- critical pedestrian/vehicle routes must be ready by **7:00 AM**, unless the Parish provides a different opening time;
- crews must maintain critical routes during continuing snowfall so accumulation does not become unsafe;
- plow operations may not leave snow blocking previously cleared sidewalks, curb cuts or entrances.

### Cemeteries
All designated interior roadways and entrances must be passable **within 36 hours after the documented end of the storm**.

Final contract must define "end of storm" using an agreed independent weather source.

Provide a funeral-priority protocol capable of accelerating clearing when a burial/service is scheduled.

## 9. Snow deicing and refreeze
BAFO must state exactly:
- what salt/ice melt is included;
- application/unit price if not included;
- labor included;
- trigger for pretreatment;
- refreeze monitoring period;
- number of included return/touch-up visits;
- documentation of material quantities.

Do not allow an accumulation charge plus duplicative T&M for ordinary work already required by that accumulation tier/seasonal band.

## 10. Extraordinary winter events
Define objective thresholds for any event eligible for T&M rather than contracted pricing.

Ordinary prolonged snowfall, routine drifting, municipal plow-in and typical refreeze should not automatically create open-ended T&M exposure.

Require:
- Parish authorization before extraordinary T&M except immediate life-safety work;
- written estimate/not-to-exceed amount;
- time/equipment logs;
- no holiday surcharge for ordinary contracted services.

## 11. Snow stacking and hauling
Attach an approved snow-stack map.

Snow hauling is not automatically authorized when a pile grows. Except for immediate safety emergencies, off-site hauling requires written Parish approval and a not-to-exceed price.

## 12. Dedicated staffing/equipment
Each vendor must show minimum committed resources by property and backup plan:
- trucks/plows;
- sidewalk machines;
- skid steers/loaders;
- salters/spreaders;
- hand crew;
- supervisor;
- backup equipment;
- expected route order.

The Parish needs to know whether municipal/commercial commitments can displace Saint Ann during a major event.

## 13. Insurance/licensing
Provide current evidence before award. Recommended requirements should be confirmed with the Parish insurer/counsel and may include:
- CGL: $1M occurrence / $2M aggregate;
- commercial auto: $1M combined single limit;
- workers compensation: statutory;
- umbrella/excess: Parish-selected limit;
- additional insured status where appropriate;
- primary/noncontributory and waiver-of-subrogation language where approved.

Chemical lawn work requires applicable Pennsylvania credentials.

## 14. Subcontractors
All subcontractors must be disclosed and approved. Prime contractor remains responsible for performance, insurance and safety.

## 15. Pricing rules
- Fixed annual/property prices must correspond to the signed maps.
- Optional-service unit rates remain fixed for the contract year.
- No automatic vendor-selected materials/pricing substitution.
- No fuel surcharge unless an objective published index, baseline, formula and cap are written into the contract.
- Prefer no surcharge; otherwise cap annual aggregate adjustment.
- All extra work requires written authorization and a stated not-to-exceed price except immediate life-safety work.

## 16. Payment
Recommended negotiating position:
- Net 30;
- no mandatory credit card on file;
- late fee no more than 1% per month or the lawful agreed maximum;
- remove 25% minimum collection penalties;
- no suspension of life-safety snow service without written notice and cure period.

## 17. Term and termination
- No evergreen renewal.
- Renewal only by written Parish authorization.
- Parish may terminate for convenience on 30 days' notice.
- Immediate/specified cure rights for safety, insurance, licensing or repeated performance failures.
- Second/third-year increases, if offered, must be stated in advance and capped (e.g., agreed CPI-U mechanism or a fixed maximum).

## 18. References and compliance package
Before final scoring, every bidder supplies:
1. completed Parish questionnaire;
2. ownership/company profile;
3. three relevant references;
4. equipment/staffing plan;
5. COI;
6. licenses/pesticide credentials;
7. complete exceptions/exclusions;
8. subcontractor list;
9. sample QC/service report;
10. signed acceptance of maps and SLAs.

## 19. Best and Final Offer format
Require one summary sheet from each bidder:
- Lawn base price by property and zone;
- natural-area credit compared with original bid;
- optional lawn services;
- snow price under light / typical / heavy modeled seasons;
- all deicing assumptions;
- maximum ordinary-season exposure;
- all T&M rates;
- all exceptions;
- 3-year pricing/renewal terms.

## 20. Award condition
Award is based on **evaluated common-scope value**, not the lowest headline total. Saint Ann reserves the right to clarify, negotiate, reference-check and reject a non-responsive BAFO.
